Chapter I · The beginning

The Call to Adventure

How I came to know board games?

Every adventure has a beginning, and mine started with curiosity. With no guide to lead me, I wandered into a board game shop and found Unmatched. The detailed illustrations and high-quality miniatures drew me in, opening the door to a hobby that would become a lasting passion.

✧
Chapter II · The fellowship

Meeting Companions

Finding friends to share the journey.

Board games became even more memorable when I found people to share them with. During my time in the student association at university, I met Ocean and Hayden. One day, I brought Unmatched to a gathering and invited them to give it a try. To my surprise, they enjoyed it just as much as I did. That single game opened the door to many more adventures, and before long, we were exploring titles like Root, Gloomhaven, and Arkham Horror together.

No adventure is meant to be undertaken alone. Gathered a party of trusted companions and created countless memories around the table.

Chapter III · The inventory

The Growing Collection

And less Money, less Space

What started with a single copy of Unmatched quickly became something much bigger. After every game I played, I found myself asking the same question: "What should I try next?" That curiosity led me down a rabbit hole of board game reviews, playthroughs, recommendations, and late-night browsing sessions.

Each new game offered a different experience. Root introduced me to asymmetric strategy, Gloomhaven opened the door to campaign-based adventures, and Arkham Horror showed me the thrill of deckbuilding and overcoming impossible odds. With every discovery, my appreciation for board game design grew.

Of course, my collection grew as well. One game became a shelf, one shelf became multiple shelves, and before long I was constantly looking for more space. My wallet may not have appreciated the journey, but my curiosity never stopped pushing me toward the next adventure waiting inside a box.

RootArtifact I

Root

Root may look cute, but it's a surprisingly deep game of strategy, negotiation, and of course betrayal. Each faction has its own unique rules and strategies, making every match feel fresh and every victory feel earned.

GloomhavenArtifact II

Gloomhaven

Gloomhaven is the game that truly introduced me to campaign board games. Every scenario feels like a challenging tactical puzzle, while the evolving story and character progression keep me invested for the long haul. From discovering new items to unlocking characters and storylines, each session feels like another chapter in an epic fantasy campaign shared with friends.

Arkham Horror LCGArtifact III

Arkham Horror LCG

Arkham Horror is a game I both love and hate. I love spending time building and refining decks, searching for the perfect combination of cards before a campaign begins. Then the game throws unimaginable horrors, impossible choices, and brutally difficult scenarios at me, reminding me that no plan survives contact with the Mythos. Yet that's exactly why I keep coming back. There is something incredibly satisfying about facing eldritch horrors with a deck I've carefully built and overcoming challenges that once seemed impossible. Every victory feels earned, and every defeat becomes a lesson for the next campaign.

Chapter V · A turning point

From Player to Retailer

New Role

What started as a search for a summer part-time job became an unexpected milestone in my board gaming journey. While browsing job listings, I came across a recruitment post from a local board game shop and decided to apply. During the interview, I shared my passion for board games with the owner, talking about the games I loved, the friends I had made, and the experiences I had around the table. Thankfully, that passion was enough. I joined the shop as a part-time retailer and found myself on the other side of the counter. Instead of looking for my next game, I was helping other players discover theirs. It felt like a full-circle moment, from a curious customer to becoming part of the community that introduced new people to the hobby.

Became the retailer. Share knowledge, recommend games, and help new players begin journeys of their own.
